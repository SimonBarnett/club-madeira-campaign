## Cast

- Priya — host; already looks after the club site
- Graham — officer who forwarded the request
- The Chair — wants a button by Thursday

## Transcript

**[hook]**

**Graham:** The committee asked for a shop. They want it on the site. By Thursday.

**Priya:** They have a spreadsheet that sells tea towels. That is not a shop.

**[escalation]**

**The Chair:** Could we just put a button? People like buttons. The treasurer has a mug that says so.

**Priya:** A button to where? An off-site portal nobody finishes, or a catalogue on the site they already have?

**[turn]**

**Graham:** We do not hold stock. We do not want another login. We just want it to look like ours.

**Priya:** Then you want a white-label catalogue on the existing site. Zero stock. I keep the client. You keep the brand.

**[button]**

**Priya:** Try the simulator. Show the committee what on-site feels like. Partner economics are on the partners page.
